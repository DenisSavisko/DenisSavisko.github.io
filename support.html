Common questions

I missed a deadline on a staked goal — can I get the charge released?
Yes, within the first 24 hours after your deadline, you can watch a couple of short ads in the Failed tab to release the hold before it's charged.

Does completing a goal on time cost anything?
No — the stake is only a hold, not a charge. Completing your goal before the deadline releases it in full, with no fee.
