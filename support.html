Common questions

Why can I only have three goals at a time?
That's the point of the app — a short list you actually finish. Complete or delete a goal to make room for the next one.

How does a friend's confirmation work?
When you add a goal, turn on "Require confirmation from someone else" and share the link. The goal can only be marked done once your friend opens the link and taps Confirm.

Do my goals sync between my devices?
Yes — goals are stored in your own iCloud account and sync to any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Does the app cost anything?
No. MyMainGoals is free, with no purchases, subscriptions, or ads.
