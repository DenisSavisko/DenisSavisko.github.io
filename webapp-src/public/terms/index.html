Stake Terms

Version 2026-10-18 · Effective October 18, 2026

These terms apply when you put money at stake on a goal in the MyMainGoals web version. MyMainGoals is operated by Denys Savisko ("we", "us"). By ticking the box when you add a staked goal, you agree to them. Using the app without stakes doesn't involve any of this.

1. Who can stake

You must be at least 18 years old, located in the United States, and using a payment card you're authorized to use.

2. How a stake works

  • When you add a staked goal, we place a hold for the amount you picked on your card through our payment processor, Stripe. Your bank shows it as a pending charge, and it reduces your available credit or balance until it's released or charged.
  • If you mark the goal done by its deadline, we release the hold. Some banks take a few days to clear a released hold from your account. That timing is up to your bank, not us.
  • If the goal isn't marked done by its deadline, it counts as missed, and the hold is charged some time after the deadline.
  • If the goal requires a friend's confirmation, the friend has to confirm it and you have to mark it done, both before the deadline. A goal your friend didn't confirm in time counts as missed, the same as any other.
  • A missed goal can't be marked done afterwards.

3. You're responsible for your goals

Keeping track of your deadlines is your responsibility. We may send you emails about a stake (for example when a hold is placed or a goal is missed), but they're a courtesy: a charge doesn't depend on any email being sent or received.

You decide whether a goal is done. Marking a goal done that you didn't actually do defeats the point of staking, but it's your call.

4. Charged stakes are non-refundable

A charged stake is the agreed result of missing your goal, not a purchase, and it is not refundable, except as described below.

  • Free release. Once every 30 days you can release one missed goal's hold before it's charged, from the app or the link in the "goal missed" email. The allowance is tied to your email address and your card.
  • Support. If something went wrong on our side, such as a release that failed because of a bug or an outage, contact us and we'll make it right. Any other refund is at our sole discretion.

5. Disputes with your bank

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank. If a stake is disputed with your bank, the email address and card used for it may be blocked from placing stakes again.

6. Limits

We may decline, limit, or stop offering stakes at any time, for example to prevent fraud. If we stop offering stakes, holds still in place are released.

7. Liability

MyMainGoals is provided "as is." To the fullest extent the law allows, our total liability to you for anything related to a stake is limited to the amount of that stake, and we're not liable for indirect or consequential losses, such as bank fees or overdraft charges caused by a hold.

8. Changes

We may update these terms. A stake is always governed by the version you agreed to when you placed it.
