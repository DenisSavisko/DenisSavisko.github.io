1. Who can stake

2. How a stake works

  • When you add a staked goal, we place a hold for the amount you picked on your card through our payment processor, Stripe. Your bank may show it as a pending charge, and it reduces your available credit or balance until it's released or charged.
  • If you mark the goal done by its deadline, we release the hold. Released holds usually disappear within a few days but can take up to about 7 business days, depending on your bank.
  • If the goal isn't marked done by its deadline, it counts as missed, and the held amount will be charged within 24 hours after the deadline (and in every case no later than 7 days after you placed the stake). If for any reason we can't charge the hold within that time, the hold is released and we will never try to charge you for that goal again.
  • If the goal requires a friend's confirmation, the friend has to confirm it and you have to mark it done, both before the deadline. A goal your friend didn't confirm in time counts as missed, the same as any other.
  • A missed goal can't be marked done afterwards.

A goal's deadline is the date and time shown when you placed the stake, in the time zone shown with it. Our server's record of when a goal was marked done is final, except that if an outage or bug on our side prevented you from marking a goal done before its deadline, we'll release the hold or refund the charge.
