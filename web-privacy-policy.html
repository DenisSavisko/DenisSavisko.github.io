Web Version Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

MyMainGoals ("the app") is a goal-tracking app that lets you optionally stake money on a goal to help you follow through. This policy explains what data the web version collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account identifier. The app signs you in anonymously (via Supabase) so friend-verification links and stakes can work. No name or password is requested.
  • Your goals. Titles, deadlines, and completion status. These sync across your own devices via your personal iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit) and are not visible to us, with two exceptions below.
  • Goals a friend confirms. For a goal you choose to have a friend confirm, its title, stake amount, and deadline are stored on our backend so the link you share can display them, and anyone holding that link can see them. Don't put sensitive information in a goal title you share with a friend.
  • Staked goals. If you stake money on a goal, we store its title, amount, deadline, and payment status; the email address you give at checkout; your time zone (so our emails show your local time); which version of the Stake Terms you agreed to; and a card fingerprint from Stripe. The fingerprint identifies the same card again without revealing its number, and is how the free release and blocks after a bank dispute are applied.
  • Declined payment attempts. If a card is declined, we record the attempt with your IP address to stop card-testing fraud. These records are deleted after two days.
  • Technical data. Our hosting and payment providers automatically receive technical data such as IP address and browser type when you use the web app; Stripe uses device and payment data for fraud prevention under its own privacy policy.

How we use your email

Only for notices about your stakes: when a hold is placed, when a goal is missed, and when a stake is charged. We don't send marketing email.

What we never collect

Your phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We never see or store your full card number. Apple Pay and Stripe handle payment details directly, end to end. Cards are not saved: you approve the card for each stake, and it's used only for that stake's hold.

Who we share data with

Only the services needed to run the app: Stripe (payment processing), Supabase (backend hosting), and Resend (sending stake emails). There are no ads, and we do not sell data to anyone.

Do Not Track

We don't track you across other websites or allow third parties to do so on our site, so we don't change our practices in response to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

How long we keep data

Stake records are kept for 7 years for tax and accounting purposes. Declined-attempt records are deleted after two days. To have your email address removed from past stake records, email support@mymaingoals.app: we'll delete it, but may keep a one-way hashed copy and the card fingerprint to apply the free-release limit and dispute blocks. Your goals live in your own iCloud account, under your control, not on our servers.

Children

MyMainGoals is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Stakes are only for people 18 and older.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a new effective date. The checkbox you tick before each stake always links to the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy: email support@mymaingoals.app, or see the Support page.
