Privacy Policy

Effective August 30, 2026

MyMainGoals ("the app") is a goal-tracking app that lets you optionally stake money on a goal to help you follow through. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account identifier. The app signs you in anonymously (via Supabase) so your data can be associated with your install — no name, email, or password is ever requested or collected.
  • Your goals. Titles, deadlines, and completion status. These sync across your own devices via your personal iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit) and are never visible to us or anyone else.
  • Staked-goal records. If you choose to stake money on a goal, the amount, deadline, and payment status are stored on our backend, linked only to your anonymous identifier — never to your real-world identity.
  • Ad requests. If you watch a rewarded ad to release a pending stake, Google AdMob serves that ad. We request non-personalized ads only, so no advertising identifier or cross-app tracking is used, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown.

What we never collect

Your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We never see or store your full card number — Apple Pay and Stripe handle payment details directly, end to end.

Who we share data with

Only the services needed to run the app: Stripe (payment processing), Supabase (backend hosting and your anonymous account), and Google AdMob (ad serving, non-personalized only). We do not sell data to anyone.

How long we keep data

Goal and stake records are kept indefinitely as your own history within the app, tied only to your anonymous identifier.

Children

MyMainGoals is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a new effective date.
