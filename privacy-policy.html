Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026

MyMainGoals ("the app") is a goal-tracking app that lets you optionally stake money on a goal to help you follow through. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account identifier. The app signs you in anonymously (via Supabase) so friend-verification links can work — no name, email, or password is requested.
  • Sign in with Apple, only if you use staking. The first time you set up staking, you sign in with Apple. We receive the stable identifier Apple issues for this app and nothing else — we do not request your name or email address, and Apple does not share your real Apple ID with us. The free goal-tracking part of the app never asks you to sign in.
  • Your goals. Titles, deadlines, and completion status. These sync across your own devices via your personal iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit) and are not visible to us. The one exception is a goal you choose to have a friend confirm: its title, stake amount, and deadline are stored on our backend so the link you share can display them, and anyone holding that link can see them.
  • Staked-goal records. If you stake money on a goal, the amount, deadline, and payment status are stored on our backend, linked to your account identifier.
  • A saved payment method, if you add one. Cards are added on this website, never inside the app. Your card details go directly to Stripe; what reaches us is only Stripe's reference to the saved card plus its brand and last four digits, so the app can show you which card is on file.
  • Ad requests. If you watch a rewarded ad to release a pending stake, Google AdMob serves that ad. We request non-personalized ads only, so no advertising identifier or cross-app tracking is used, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown.

What we never collect

Your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We never see or store your full card number — Apple Pay and Stripe handle payment details directly, end to end.

Who we share data with

Only the services needed to run the app: Stripe (payment processing and storing your saved card), Supabase (backend hosting and your account), and Google AdMob (ad serving, non-personalized only). We do not sell data to anyone.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings inside the app. Doing so removes your sign-in, your saved card (deleted at Stripe as well), and any pending card-linking data. Any stake still being held is released first, so you are not charged for it. Records of stakes that have already completed are kept as a financial record, with your identifier removed so they are no longer connected to you. Your goals are not stored on our servers — they live in your own iCloud account and stay there, under your control, whether or not you delete your account here.

How long we keep data

Stake records are kept indefinitely as a financial record. Everything else is removed when you delete your account, as described above.

Children

MyMainGoals is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a new effective date.
