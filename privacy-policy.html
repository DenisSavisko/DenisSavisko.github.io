Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

This policy covers the MyMainGoals iOS app ("the app"), a goal-tracking app. It explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • An anonymous identifier. The app signs in anonymously (via Supabase) so friend-confirmation links can work. No name, email, or password is ever requested, and there is no account to create.
  • Your goals. Titles, deadlines, and completion status. These sync across your own devices via your personal iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit) and are not visible to us.
  • Goals you ask a friend to confirm. If you turn on friend confirmation for a goal, its title and deadline are stored on our backend so the link you share can display them. Anyone holding that link can see them and confirm the goal.

What we never collect

Your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any payment information. The app contains no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

Who we share data with

Only Supabase, which hosts the backend for friend-confirmation links. We do not sell or share data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

Deleting a goal in the app removes it from your iCloud. To have a friend-confirmation record removed from our backend, contact us via the Support page.

Children

MyMainGoals is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the Support page.
